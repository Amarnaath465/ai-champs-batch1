# Module 01 — Guided Lab: AI Champions Kick-off & Embedded Engineering Enterprise Context

**Duration:** 1.5 hours | **Format:** Facilitated workshop (no coding) | **Audience:** All roles — Architects, Developers, Testers, DevOps/SRE, Engineering Leads, and selected Program/Product roles

This is a **workshop-format lab**, not a coding lab. Module 01 establishes the enterprise context, the AI Champion role, and the shared vocabulary the rest of the 12-module programme builds on — before GitHub Copilot, Spec-Driven Development, or Agent Prism are opened. Two concentrated exercises produce everything you carry forward into later modules.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lab, you will have:

1. Surfaced and prioritized your team's real delivery pain points, and named the risks unmanaged AI usage adds to them.
2. Placed yourself in the AI Champion / Evangelist / Sponsor model.
3. Produced a **Current-State → Target-State workflow canvas** with success metrics for your team/GBE.
4. Selected **one greenfield and one brownfield use case** that carry forward into Module 05.

## Materials Needed

- The two exercise files (printed, or a shared doc/Miro/Mural board per group)

## Deliverables Checklist

Walk out of Module 01 with:

- [ ] A prioritized top-5 pain-point list, tagged by lifecycle stage and risk (Exercise 1)
- [ ] One risk flagged for governance follow-up (Exercise 1)
- [ ] One Current-State → Target-State workflow canvas per GBE, with success-metrics baselines (Exercise 2)

Keep these artifacts — they are direct inputs to Module 05 (Greenfield/Brownfield SDLC), Module 09 (PR Quality/LLM-as-Judge), Module 10 (Agent Prism), and Module 11 (ROI & Token Economics).
